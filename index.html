01 — NOVELTY

The novelty isn't a component. It's the architecture.

Conventional kits bolt sensing and control onto a rapidly spinning shell. This proposal removes the constraint instead of compensating for it — separating a low-spin guidance nose from the spin-stabilized carrier, then coupling power, perception, and coordination around that one decision.

04 — ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS

Six mutually reinforcing subsystems

01Despun Nose ArchitectureDNA
Mechanically isolated guidance section providing a stable reference frame for sensing and control after muzzle exit.
02Liquid Mass Transfer SteeringLMTS
Internal redistribution of dense working mass for coarse steering moments, reducing reliance on exposed external actuators.
03Event-Driven Terminal SeekerEDTS
Low-latency neuromorphic-vision concept for terminal recognition when satellite navigation is degraded.
04Spin-Harvesting Axial-Flux GeneratorSHAF-G
Integrated generator converting relative shell–nose rotation into electrical power for transient loads.
05Cooperative Swarm Mesh NetworkCSMN
Salvo-level coordination layer sharing navigation and environmental estimates across nodes.
06Metamaterial Conformal Multi-Function ApertureMCMA
Structural nose concept combining radome, antenna, shielding, and load-bearing functions in one surface.

06 — SYSTEM SYNERGY

Removing bottlenecks, not stacking features

DNA → EDTS / SHAF-G / MCMA
Stable sensing reference, usable relative rotation, better antenna integration.
SHAF-G → EDTS / CSMN
Energy buffer for perception and communications loads.
MCMA → CSMN / LMTS
More internal volume and integrated EM interfaces.
LMTS → DNA / fine control
Coarse correction with less exposed actuation.
EDTS → Terminal guidance
Observation beyond a single navigation source.
CSMN → All functions
Shared state estimation and graceful degradation.

12 — APPENDIX

One-page judge pitch

PROBLEM
High-dynamics spinning platforms force sensing, control, power, and comms to share one difficult reference frame.
SOLUTION
Separate the guidance reference frame, integrate major functions into the nose, coordinate through phased control and cooperative estimation.
PROOF
CAD, 6-DOF simulation, rotating-interface power test, event-based perception, multi-node estimator.
NOVELTY
A spin-stabilized body with a stable, self-powered, sensor-rich, network-aware guidance layer — the innovation is the synergy, not any one part.
