01 — NOVELTY

The novelty isn't a component. It's the architecture.

Conventional kits bolt sensing and control onto a rapidly spinning shell. This proposal removes the constraint instead of compensating for it — separating a low-spin guidance nose from the spin-stabilized carrier, then coupling power, perception, and coordination around that one decision.

SAFETY BOUNDARY — Inert laboratory demonstrators, simulation, environmental sensing, and controlled shutdown only. No explosive design, no engagement logic, no operational deployment guidance. Full text in the research proposal.
02 — RESEARCH FOUNDATION

One proposal, seven pillars, seven testable phases

The full 13-section research proposal (IPGA-COMBINED-001 v2.0) defines the design thesis, the evidence plan, and the safety case this website demonstrates. Thesis in one sentence: separate the sensing reference frame, recover useful energy from the motion that remains, fuse asynchronous observations conservatively, and end with safe environmental observation after a controlled event.

§2–§4
Design thesis & pillars
Reference-frame transition (locked → despun), then mission-purpose transition (guidance → environmental monitoring). Seven pillars: DNA, LMTS, EDTS, SHAF-G, CSMN, MCMA, IDSES.
§3
Concept of operations
Seven real-time phases: initialization → locked surrogate → despin study → nav handover → cooperative estimation → impact surrogate → monitoring & shutdown.
§5
Evidence plan
Eight work packages — CAD, 6-DOF Monte Carlo, rotating-rig tests, event perception, mesh fault-injection, shock survival, deployment, hardware-in-the-loop. Every claim ships with uncertainty.
§6–§7
Risks, safety, roadmap
Risk register with the despin interface as critical path, NIST-aligned AI risk framing, inert-only interlocks, and a 6-stage SIH demonstration roadmap.

04 — ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS

Seven mutually reinforcing subsystems

01Despun Nose ArchitectureDNA
Mechanically isolated guidance section providing a stable reference frame for sensing and control after muzzle exit.
02Liquid Mass Transfer SteeringLMTS
Internal redistribution of dense working mass for coarse steering moments, reducing reliance on exposed external actuators.
03Event-Driven Terminal SeekerEDTS
Low-latency neuromorphic-vision concept for terminal recognition when satellite navigation is degraded.
04Spin-Harvesting Axial-Flux GeneratorSHAF-G
Integrated generator converting relative shell–nose rotation into electrical power for transient loads.
05Cooperative Swarm Mesh NetworkCSMN
Salvo-level coordination layer sharing navigation and environmental estimates across nodes.
06Metamaterial Conformal Multi-Function ApertureMCMA
Structural nose concept combining radome, antenna, shielding, and load-bearing functions in one surface.
07Impact-Survivable Deployable Environmental SensorIDSES
Protected sensor carrier that survives a controlled impact surrogate, deploys a sensing head on passing self-check, monitors bounded environmental categories, then shuts down safely. Demonstrated live in Shell Strike below.

06 — SYSTEM SYNERGY

Removing bottlenecks, not stacking features

DNA → EDTS / SHAF-G / MCMA
Stable sensing reference, usable relative rotation, better antenna integration.
SHAF-G → EDTS / CSMN
Energy buffer for perception and communications loads.
MCMA → CSMN / LMTS
More internal volume and integrated EM interfaces.
LMTS → DNA / fine control
Coarse correction with less exposed actuation.
EDTS → Terminal guidance
Observation beyond a single navigation source.
CSMN → All functions
Shared state estimation and graceful degradation.
P1–P6 → IDSES carrier
Stable, powered, networked platform delivers the sensor to its post-event mission.
IDSES → Safe demonstration
Bounded sensing, status packets, predictable shutdown — proof without harmful function.
HIL → All transitions
Fault injection across mode changes; no unsafe state reachable.

10 — APPENDIX

One-page judge pitch

PROBLEM
High-dynamics spinning platforms force sensing, control, power, and comms to share one difficult reference frame.
SOLUTION
Separate the guidance reference frame, integrate major functions into the nose, coordinate through phased control and cooperative estimation.
PROOF
CAD, 6-DOF simulation, six runnable browser simulations (eddy damping, dynamo, ballast, sensor deployment, two GLB viewers), rotating-interface power test, multi-node estimator.
NOVELTY
A spin-stabilized body with a stable, self-powered, sensor-rich, network-aware guidance layer — the innovation is the synergy, not any one part.
