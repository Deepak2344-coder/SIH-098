EDDY BRAKE

Three-dimensional eddy-current braking system. A slotted aluminium ring spins with the drum; engaging the button magnet drives eddy currents in the ring, and Lenz’s law produces a braking torque that drags the rotor down to a creep — the kinetic energy leaves as heat.
